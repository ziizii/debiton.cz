This policy explains how we process the personal data you provide to us through the www.debiton.cz website, in particular through the form for uploading unpaid invoices and the contact form, and what rights you have.

## 2. What data we process

- **Contact details** – the e-mail address and/or phone number you enter in the form and the content of your comment or message; in the contact form also your name and company name, if you provide them.
- **Uploaded documents** – invoices, contracts, orders, correspondence, etc. They may contain personal data about you, your employees and your debtors (e.g. name, address, company ID, contact details, details of the amount owed).
- **Technical data** – IP address and information about your browser and your behaviour on the page, which Google reCAPTCHA evaluates to protect the form against abuse.

## 3. Why we process the data and on what legal basis

- **Handling your enquiry and assessing the claim** – the processing is necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). Where the data relate to your representatives or employees, the legal basis is our legitimate interest in communicating with your company (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
- **Collecting the claim** – we process debtors’ data on the basis of the legitimate interest in asserting and recovering the claim (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
- **Protecting the website against spam and abuse** – legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
- **Compliance with legal obligations** – once a contract is concluded, in particular obligations under accounting and tax legislation (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR).

## 4. Who we share the data with

- **Providers of technical and administrative services** who process the data for us as processors, in particular Google (Google Cloud – file storage and e-mail; reCAPTCHA), and a provider of administrative support.
- **The cooperating law firm**, which assesses the claim and, on behalf of your company, sends reminders and letters before action to debtors, files applications for payment orders and lodges claims in insolvency proceedings. The law firm then processes the data as an independent controller.
- **Courts, court bailiffs, insolvency administrators and other authorities**, where necessary to recover the claim.

Google services may involve transfers of data to the USA. Google is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, and transfers are further safeguarded by standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission.

## 5. How long we keep the data

- Files uploaded through the form are automatically deleted from storage no later than 30 days after upload.
- We keep your enquiry and related communication while it is being handled. If no contract is concluded, for no longer than 3 years after the last contact.
- If a contract is concluded, we keep the data for its duration and then for the period necessary to protect our rights and comply with legal obligations, for no longer than 10 years.

## 6. Your rights

You have the right to request access to your personal data, its rectification or erasure, restriction of processing and data portability. You may object at any time to processing based on legitimate interest. You can exercise your rights by e-mail at {{< email >}}.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů), Pplk. Sochora 27, 170 00 Prague 7, <a href="https://uoou.gov.cz" rel="noopener">uoou.gov.cz</a>.

## 7. Cookies

The website does not use analytics or marketing cookies. When you use the form, the Google reCAPTCHA service is loaded, which may store necessary data and cookies to detect automated abuse.

## 8. Debtors’ data

If you are a debtor and we obtained your data from a creditor, we process it to assert the claim on the basis of legitimate interest. You also have all the rights listed above, including the right to object.
